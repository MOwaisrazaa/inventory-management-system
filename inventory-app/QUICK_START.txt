🐛 TROUBLESHOOTING

SOLUTION: Composer install complete hone ka wait karo

SOLUTION: E:\xampp\php\php.exe artisan key:generate

PROBLEM: "SQLSTATE[HY000]: General error: 1030"
SOLUTION: Database created hai check karo

PROBLEM: "Port 8000 already in use"
SOLUTION: E:\xampp\php\php.exe artisan serve --port=8001
